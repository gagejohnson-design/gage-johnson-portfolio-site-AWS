💼 My Role

I support large enterprise software implementations involving complex data models, system integrations, and cross-functional teams.

My work focuses heavily on the data layer of these implementations, ensuring systems launch with trusted data and stable architecture.

  • Designing and validating ETL workflows used during system migrations
  • Mapping legacy data structures into modern enterprise platform models
  • Supporting master data governance across operational systems
  • Coordinating with engineers and architects to resolve technical issues during implementation
  • Leading structured data validation and QA cycles before go-live

These implementations often span multiple business units and involve large volumes of operational data. The focus is always the same: ensure the system launches with accurate data and predictable behavior.

Managers and project leaders rely on me to bring structure, preparation, and technical clarity to high-stakes delivery environments.
